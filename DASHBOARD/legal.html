LEGAL / NOTICES

System boundaries and usage notices.

Autonomous Smart Space Debris Capture System (ADCS-01) is an academic simulation and research demonstrator.

SIMULATION STATUS

Non-flight system

The website, FOC and orbital simulation represent conceptual system behaviour. They are not flight-qualified software, live mission control, safety-critical systems or spacecraft authorization interfaces.

DATA STATUS

Reference and synthetic values

Displayed positions, velocities, ranges, scores and mission states are simulation/reference values unless explicitly connected to a local project data source. They must not be interpreted as live spacecraft telemetry.

AFFILIATION

No agency affiliation

This project is not produced, operated, endorsed or certified by NASA, ISRO, ESA or another government space agency. The visual and terminology choices are intended to follow familiar mission-control conventions without implying institutional ownership.

ASSETS

Local project media

The welcome video and Phase 1–15 figures are project-supplied assets. Third-party libraries, textures, fonts and models remain subject to their respective licenses and terms.
