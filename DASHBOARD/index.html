Autonomous Space Debris Monitoring & Capture System

A conceptual spacecraft system for detecting, tracking and assessing orbital debris before carrying out a controlled rendezvous and capture sequence. The project combines orbital data processing, mission decision logic, simulation and a laboratory-scale robotic capture interface.

MISSION PURPOSE

The system is built around a simple operational chain.

The spacecraft first establishes an orbital picture, obtains a target state, checks the conditions for close approach and only then permits the capture sequence to proceed.

01

OBSERVE

Collect orbital and relative-state information.

02

ESTIMATE

Build a usable position and velocity record.

03

DECIDE

Check risk, feasibility and available margin.

04

APPROACH

Control the proximity sequence.

05

CAPTURE

Secure, transfer and contain the object.

SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE

Four engineering layers keep the interfaces clear.

01

ORBITAL DATA

Catalog data, state extraction, proximity filtering and multi-object tracking.

02

MISSION LOGIC

Prediction, risk assessment, target selection and state-machine control.

03

SIMULATION

Earth-centred visualization, relative motion and capture behaviour.

04

PHYSICAL INTERFACE

Local capture coordinates, inverse kinematics, serial commands and prototype actuation.
